# 🧴 SMART DOSE — Consumer Behavior Analytics Dashboard

> A B2B data analytics tool that helps FMCG brands quantify the cost of liquid soap overuse — and build a data-backed case for change.

---

## 💡 The Problem

Most consumers use 2–3x more liquid soap than needed — not because they want to waste, but because dosage is never clearly communicated. Consumers don't think about "correct dose" the way they think about cleanliness or quality.

This silent waste costs consumers money, increases plastic consumption, and quietly damages the brand's sustainability footprint.

**The question we asked:** *Can data prove this is happening — and show brands exactly what it's costing them?*

---

## 📊 What the Data Says (91 Responses)

| Finding | % |
|---|---|
| Consumers who don't know the correct dose | 37% |
| Consumers who knowingly use more than recommended | 31% |
| Consumers who dilute the product (unclear instructions signal) | 61% |
| Consumers who said clearer dosage would reduce their usage | 40% |
| Consumers who said "maybe" clearer dosage would help | 33% |
| Consumers using more than 2 pumps per wash | 25% |
| Consumers washing dishes more than twice daily | 58% |

> **Key insight:** The problem is not consumer behavior — it is the absence of a dosage standard in the product design itself. This is a packaging and communication problem, which means it is fixable.

---

## 🎯 Who Is This For?

SMART DOSE is built for **FMCG brand managers and product teams** who want to:
- Quantify how much revenue is being lost to overuse
- Identify which consumer segments are highest risk
- Make a data-backed business case for changing packaging or dosage communication
- Use sustainability data as a positive marketing asset

---

## 📈 How Each Page Helps the Business Owner

### 1. 📊 Overview — *"What is happening with my product?"*
Shows the full picture of consumer usage behavior: risk levels, dilution patterns, packaging satisfaction, and instruction clarity. Gives the brand manager a quick health check on how their product is actually being used in real households.

### 2. 👥 Segmentation — *"Who are my consumers and what drives them?"*
Breaks consumers into behavioral segments from highest to lowest overuse risk. Helps marketing teams craft targeted communication — for example, high-risk segments need clearer on-pack dosage; low-risk segments can be used as testimonials.

### 3. ⚠️ Waste Score — *"How exposed is my brand to waste risk?"*
Scores the brand's overuse exposure across 4 factors: quantity per wash, frequency of use, dilution awareness, and dosage knowledge. A high waste score means the brand is producing more product than consumers actually need — which is hidden lost profit.

### 4. 💰 Growth Opportunity — *"What is this costing me — and what can I recover?"*
The business case page. The brand manager enters their own numbers:
- **Product price (EGP)**
- **Bottle size (ml)**
- **Number of customers**
- **Production cost (EGP)**

The dashboard calculates the current revenue lost to overuse and the potential profit recoverable if dosage behavior improves — giving the brand a clear ROI for investing in dosage clarity.

### 5. 🌱 Environmental Impact — *"How do I turn this problem into a marketing advantage?"*
Translates overuse data into environmental numbers: water savings, plastic bottle reduction, and CO₂ estimates. These figures can be used directly in sustainability marketing campaigns, ESG reports, and green brand positioning — turning a problem into a competitive advantage.

---

## 🔑 Key Recommendation

> Fixing dosage clarity is not a cost — it is a revenue recovery strategy. 73% of consumers said clearer dosage guidance would reduce or possibly reduce their usage. That is a measurable, addressable market. The brand that acts first owns the "responsible consumption" narrative in its category.

## 💡 Additional Recommendations

### 1. Packaging Design
The wide bottle opening is a silent overuse driver — product pours freely before the consumer makes a conscious decision. **Recommendation:** standardized pump or controlled-opening design that limits each dispense to the correct dose automatically.

### 2. On-Pack Dosage Label
"Use an appropriate amount" is not a dosage instruction. **Recommendation:** specific ml or pump count printed clearly on the label — the same way medicine dosage is communicated. This removes ambiguity at the point of use.

### 3. Concentrated Formula Opportunity
61% of consumers dilute the product with water — this is not just a waste signal, it is a product insight. Consumers are already creating their own "concentrated formula." **Recommendation:** launch a concentrated variant at a premium price point, positioned around sustainability and value. This turns a problem behavior into a revenue opportunity.

## ⚠️ Limitations & Honest Notes

### Sample Size
The dataset includes synthetic data generated to supplement 31–33 real survey responses. While the patterns are directionally valid, the findings should be treated as a **proof of concept** rather than statistically significant conclusions. A larger real-world sample would be needed before making product or packaging decisions.

### Scope
All survey questions focused on dish soap specifically. Results may not be directly applicable to other liquid soap categories (hand soap, shampoo, etc.) without separate validation.

### Missing Demographics
The dataset does not include demographic variables (age, income, location). This limits the depth of segmentation and makes it harder to prioritize which consumer group to target first.

### ROI Assumptions
The Growth Opportunity calculations are based on inputs provided by the brand — the model is only as accurate as the numbers entered. Assumptions about waste percentage and behavioral change rate should be validated with real sales data before being used in financial planning.
